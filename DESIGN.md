# Porridge design

The look follows Things 3: quiet white pages, big bold titles, thin lines, and color that says what something is. This file replaces the direction chosen in `design-demos/direction-approved.md` where the app has moved on. iOS and Mac follow the same rules; colors and sizes live in `Porridge/Design/Theme.swift`.

## Color

| Color | Value | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sky | `#24B4E9`, text `#0B79A8` | The plus button, selection, toggles, the mic button, notes and their date headers |
| Navy | `#1F3667`, dark `#B0C4EC` | Titles of notes, icons on bars and buttons, symbols on sky, orange and green, meetings, Archive, pins |
| Green | `#29BD80`, text `#0F8A5A` | To-dos and their box, check boxes in summaries |
| Violet | `#7C5CD6`, dark `#9B82E8` | Bowls (projects in the code) |
| Orange | `#FA8F24`, text `#B55D00` | Saved links |
| Red | System red, text `#D93025`, dark `#FF6B60` | Recently Deleted, everything that deletes, recording (the stop button and the mic while it runs), and the due day of an open to-do that is late |

Speakers have their own sequence of colors (`speakerColors`), starting with sky and orange. It says who is talking and nothing about the kind of thing.

Every kind of thing keeps its color everywhere: in the list icon, its section header and its page.

Sky, orange and green are for surfaces. As text or a symbol on a white page they use the darker "text" value, which reaches 4.5:1 (green 3:1, used only bold). Symbols on a full fill of them are navy, never white. In dark mode the bright values are the readable ones. The Apple platforms also have a variant for Increase Contrast.

## Layout

- **Pages** are white (near black in dark mode) with hairlines as separators. Note pages scroll as a whole, head included, on iPhone. The Mac keeps the head of a note in place.
- **Moving between pages:** on iPhone and iPad pages slide in and out as the system does it, and going back follows the finger where the platform offers that. The Mac sidebar changes in place.
- **Three places:** Today, Inbox and Bowls. The place Bowls as a whole shows a bowl of the app's own (`Places.bowlsSymbol`: template SVGs `BowlPlaceFill`/`BowlPlace`; SF Symbols has no bowl): the tab (outlined, filled while chosen), the Bowls page's head square, the Go To row of the search, What's New and the onboarding, and on the Mac the sidebar row, the list head, the command bar's Go to Bowls, View › Bowls and the Bowls place's empty symbol. A single bowl (its row, page head, the bowl of an entry, menus to move into one) keeps the folder; so does the Shortcuts app's tile (App Shortcuts take SF Symbols only). iPhone: the tab bar Today · Inbox · **+** · Bowls · Search (badges: Today the to-dos due, Inbox what waits), one stack per tab. The bar is drawn by the app (`PlaceTabBar`, a floating glass capsule like the system's, which is hidden): the system's five even slots left no room for a big "+". "+" in the middle is no tab: a raised round button, 64 pt (58 pt on screens narrower than 390 pt), taller than the bar and rising 12 pt (10 pt) above its top edge, a deeper sky toward its bottom with a navy "+" and a soft shadow. Its middle slot is the circle and 14 pt (13 pt) on each side, the four tabs share the rest, so the Inbox's selection capsule and a badge "120" end before the circle, also at 375 pt (`TabBarLayout`). Tapping the tab in front takes its stack back to its start. Symbols, names and "+" do not grow with the text size (large content viewer); the bar hides while choosing several (the selection bar takes its place), Triage, sheets and the keyboard cover it. The pages keep the bar and the circle free at the bottom (lists scroll under it), so the last rows and the Inbox's end card are reachable above "+". Every launch starts on Today (a link, widget, shortcut or notification moves on from there); coming back from the background keeps the tab. Mac: the sidebar Today ⌘1 · Inbox ⌘2 · Bowls ⌘3 (unfolding into every bowl) · Chat ⌘4, then Smart Filters, Archive ⌘5 and Recently Deleted ⌘6, always shown; the main window starts on Today at every launch (its size and place come back). - **Kinds:** to-dos, meetings, notes, links, images and files. Images and files are two kinds that behave alike. A bowl's filter pills show only the kinds it holds.

## Controls

- **Sheets:** "Cancel" on the left when closing throws work away, "Done" on the right when nothing is lost.
- **Deleting for good always asks first.** Moving something to Recently Deleted does not, because it can come back.
- **Main buttons** are a wash of their color with a navy label. Disabled buttons fade; destructive ones turn red.
- **Summary models** are one list, on-device and cloud alike, with one default. Adding one happens in a sheet; a meeting chooses its model and template in one sheet, and that choice holds for this summary only.
- **Menus** are grouped by a line: the note and its summary, the recording, what deletes.
- **Native controls first.** Custom ones only where the system has no equivalent.
- **Wording:** English Title Case for buttons and titles, "…" without a space before it.

## To-dos

A to-do is a note like any other, not a list of its own: it lives in the timeline, the Inbox, bowls, Recently Deleted, the search, the chat and the export the same way. What makes it a to-do is a done state; its subtasks are the checklist lines of its text. (The to-dos removed in 1.1 were items of their own with a tab of their own; that parallel world does not come back.)

**Using them**

- **Creating:** "To-do" in the Add sheet makes a new note that is a to-do, the same way as "Note" (in a bowl it goes into it). Any note that is no meeting and no saved link or file can be turned into a to-do and back: "Convert to To-do" / "Convert to Note" in the note's More menu and in the long press of its row. The text stays as it is.
- **Icon and box:** a to-do has the icon of its kind like every entry (green rounded square, `checklist`), the same whether it is open, done or late: the state is the box's and the due day's, not the icon's. The box that ticks it is a separate control at the end of the row, 26 pt drawn and 44 pt to tap: open a green outline with a light green wash, done filled green with a navy `checkmark`. On its page the same box stands before the title. Icon and box are apart because the icon of every other kind opens the entry rather than changing it, and an empty box at the start of a row read as a missing icon.
- **Row:** tapping the box ticks the to-do right in the list. A done one is struck through and grey. The row's preview shows the open subtasks (joined by " · "), its footer the progress "2/5" with a `checkmark.circle` symbol, like the circles of the subtasks (green once all are done). Swipe from the left and the long press have "Mark as Done" / "Mark as Not Done".
- **Page:** the box stands before the title; the bar says "To-do" in green. The meta line adds "Done …" and "2 of 5 done". The body is the normal editor; its circles are the subtasks: tap to tick, Return for the next, Tab to indent, the format menu moves a line up or down ("Move Line Up/Down", ⌥⌘↑/↓ on a keyboard), deleting the line deletes the subtask. "Add Subtask" below the text adds an empty one at the end with the cursor in it.
- **Done:** a done to-do stays where it is, ticked and grey, for the rest of the day. Done to-dos are never archived: from the next day on an older done one stays in its bowl (bowl view: flat list by last change, pinned on top, the filter "To-dos" by due date) or in the Inbox when it has no bowl. "Mark as Not Done" opens it again. The Archive is only for bowls; single notes cannot be archived.
- **Due day:** a to-do can be due on a day (no time of day). Its page has a chip first in the row of bowls and tags: "+ Due Date" while there is none, otherwise the day ("Today", "Fri", "12 Dec") in green, or in red when it is late. The chip is a menu: Today, Tomorrow, Next Week (the first day of next week, as the calendar counts weeks), Pick Date… (the system's calendar in a sheet), and Clear Due Date. The long press of a list row has the same menu ("Due Date"). A new to-do sets it the same way, right on its page. In the row the day stands in the footer with a calendar symbol: red and semibold when an open to-do is late (from midnight after the day, with a calendar symbol with an exclamation mark), green and semibold when it is due today, grey otherwise; a done to-do is never marked. "Make To-do" from a summary sets no day. There are no reminders or notifications and nothing goes to a calendar.
- **Filter:** the search has the chip "Open To-dos" (to-dos not done), grouped by due day: Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No Due Date (empty groups left out), each by due day. A bowl has the filter pill "To-dos" and the section "To-dos" first: open ones by due day (earliest first, those without one after them), then those done today; its row counts "Open To-dos". Everywhere else the timeline stays as it is.
- **From a meeting (suggested to-dos):** see the section below. The single "Make To-do" button on each task line is gone: the suggestions replace it. A task line that became a to-do wears the to-do symbol (`checklist`, green) at its end.

**Format**

- Fields of `Note`, all optional, nil/absent by default:
  - `todoStateRaw: String?` — `"open"`, `"done"`, or `"none"` for a to-do turned back into a note. Absent (nil) = the note never was a to-do. `isTodo` = open or done; `isDone` = done.
  - `todoDoneAt: Date?` — when it was ticked; nil while open.
  - `todoSourceID: String?` — the `id` (uppercase UUID) of the note whose summary task it came from.
  - `todoDueDay: String?` — the due day as `"yyyy-MM-dd"` (zero-padded, Gregorian): a day of the calendar, no time, no time zone, so it is the same day on every device wherever it is. `"none"` = a due day was taken away. Nil = never set. It counts only while the note is a to-do (`todoDue`); a note turned back keeps it stored and does not show it. Anything that is not ten characters of three numbers counts as no due day.
- **Subtasks:** lines matching `^([ \t]*)[-*+][ \t]+\[([ xX])\](?:[ \t]+(.*))?$` (the editor's checklist syntax), outside ```` ``` ```` code blocks, with non-empty text after trimming. Ticked = `x` or `X`. Indent = (spaces + 2 per tab) / 2. The app writes `- [ ] ` and `- [x] `, two spaces per indent level. Progress = ticked / all; "complete" needs at least one subtask.
- **Done rules** (on a change of the subtasks from old to new text, `Todos.stateAfterEdit`): open and the new text complete while the old was not → done (`todoDoneAt` = now). Done and the new text has an open subtask while the old had none, or fewer are ticked than before → open (`todoDoneAt` = nil). Nothing else changes the state: reopening a to-do by hand leaves its subtasks ticked and it stays open. Ticking the to-do itself ticks all its subtasks; unticking it leaves them.
- **Due day rules:** today = the device's current day in its time zone, as `"yyyy-MM-dd"`. Keys compare as strings. Overdue = open to-do and due day < today. Due today = due day == today. Labels (`Todos.dueLabel`): days from today 0 "Today", 1 "Tomorrow", -1 "Yesterday", 2 to 6 the abbreviated weekday (template `EEE`), otherwise day and abbreviated month (template `dMMM`) in this year, with the year (template `dMMMy`) in another; formatted in the device's locale. Sorting (`Todos.byDue`): open to-dos by due day ascending, those without a due day after them in their previous order, then the done ones. Groups (`Todos.dueGroups`): Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No Due Date.
- **Put away:** `isPutAway` = done and `todoDoneAt` before the start of today, local time. It only decides the section "Done Today" of the To-dos list; it does not move or hide the to-do anywhere else (Today leaves out every done to-do, by its state, not by this). `Note.archivedAt` stays in the model only for compatibility and is cleared once at launch; notes are no longer archived individually.
- **Chat:** a to-do adds one passage to the chat index before its summary and text: `To-do, open: <title>` or `To-do, done on <day month year>: <title>`; with a due day `, due <d MMMM yyyy in English, e.g. 12 December 2026>` follows the state, and `, overdue` after it when it is open and late; then `, <n> of <m> subtasks done` when it has subtasks, then the colon and the title (`ChatRetrieval.todoLine`). The passage is dated by the start of its due day (other passages by the note's creation), so a question about a time ("this week") finds what is due then. The index hash of a to-do includes `todoStateRaw`, `todoDueDay`, `todoDoneAt` and whether it is overdue, so the passage is cut again when one of them changes (also at midnight).
- **Export:** `exportText` adds the line `[ ] To-do` or `[x] To-do, done` after the head, with `, due <d MMMM yyyy>` when it has a due day.

## Suggested to-dos

The tasks of a summary are offered as to-dos; nothing becomes a to-do without a tap. Meetings and notes with a summary have them, saved links and files never (their summaries have no tasks).

**Using them**

- **Where:** after a summary is written, the summary tab of the page shows a card "Suggested To-dos" first, above the summary: a green wash with a thin green border, the heading with `checklist` in green. Each open task is a line with a selection circle (`checkmark.circle.fill` green / `circle` grey), its title as an editable text field, and under it the owner (`person`) and the due day (`calendar`, same labels as everywhere) when the summary names them.
- **Picked at first:** the user's own tasks and those without an owner, when the app knows the user's name (Settings, "You", or the voice marked as the user's); all of them when it does not. Somebody else's task stays unpicked, because a meeting usually gives the user a few tasks and the others theirs.
- **Taking them on:** the button "Add 3 To-dos" (the count of picked ones; "Add 1 To-do"; disabled at 0) makes one open to-do per picked task: title = the (edited) task, in the same bowls as the meeting (`filedAt` set when there are any), `todoSourceID` = the meeting, `todoDueDay` = the named day, and text `Owner: <name>` when it is somebody else's task. Its long press has "Add as One To-do with Subtasks": one to-do "Follow-ups: <meeting title>" with the picked tasks as `- [ ] ` lines (`<task> (<owner>)` for somebody else's), due on the earliest named day. The tasks not picked are dismissed at the same time, so the card is done.
- **Dismiss** next to it dismisses all the tasks of the card.
- **Back again:** "Suggest To-dos" in the page's More menu (with a summary, not for a to-do or a saved page) brings the dismissed tasks back and shows the summary tab. Taken ones stay taken.
- **Made to-dos:** below the summary a list "To-dos" shows the to-dos made from the note (by `todoSourceID`), open by due day first, each with its box (ticked right there), title, due day and a chevron; a tap opens it. The to-do's page has the pill back to the meeting.
- **Each task once:** the summary on the page leaves out the task lines that the card or the list of made to-dos shows (the card's suggestions; a made to-do's title or a subtask of the one to-do with subtasks, by key or same task; a task taken under an edited title while any made to-do is there), and the tasks heading (`## Action Items`, `### Aufgaben`, `**Next Steps:**`, … as `SummaryTasks` reads them) when nothing but a "No further tasks." line is left under it (`Note.summaryShown`, `SummaryTasks.hiding`). Dismissed and ticked tasks, and every task while the card is not shown (locked or read-only page), stay in the summary, since nothing else shows them. Display only: the lines are blanked so ticking a box still finds its line; the stored summary, Copy, Insert into Note, export, chat and MCP keep all its tasks. The prompt is unchanged, because the suggestions are read from it.
- **List row:** a note with waiting suggestions says "3 suggested to-dos" (`checklist`, green text) in its footer ("1 suggested to-do"), left out where the row is narrow.

**Format**

- The summary prompt asks for task lines as `- [ ] <task, a short instruction clear on its own> – <owner, if known> – <due date as YYYY-MM-DD, only if a date or deadline was named>`, owner and date left out when not named (a note's summary: `- [ ] <task> – <due date as YYYY-MM-DD, if named>`). No extra model call: the suggestions are read from the summary.
- **Tasks** (`TodoSuggestions.parse`): the open task lines of `SummaryTasks.parse` (ticked ones are not offered). The text is split at ` – ` or ` — ` (or ` - ` when there is neither): the first part is the title (`**` and leading or trailing dashes and colons removed), each further part trimmed of spaces, brackets, `*_.,;` and dashes is a due day if it reads as one, otherwise the owner (the first part without a digit), unless it is a placeholder (`-`, `?`, `n/a`, `none`, `unknown`, `tbd`, `not named`, `keine`, `niemand`, `unbekannt`, `offen`, `nicht genannt`, …, anything starting with `<` or containing "if known", "if named", "falls"). Each task once, by key.
- **Due day** (`TodoSuggestions.dueDay`, relative to the note's `createdAt` in the device's calendar): `yyyy-m-d`; `d.m.yyyy`, `d.m.yy` (20yy) or `d.m.`; a day and a month name (`28 November`, `28. Nov.`, `3 décembre`, `4 de diciembre de 2026`) or a month name and a day (`November 28, 2026`), month names and short forms in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese and Polish. Without a year: the first such day on or after the meeting's day. Invalid days and words without a date ("next Friday", "end of the month", "in 2 weeks") give none.
- **Key** (`TodoSuggestions.key`): the title case- and diacritic-folded, split at everything that is not a letter or digit, joined by single spaces ("Prüfe Ölstand!" → `prufe olstand`). **Same task** (`isSame`): equal keys, or both with three words or more and at least 60 % of the union of their words shared (Jaccard), so a summary written again keeps what was decided.
- **Mine:** no owner, or one of the owner's names (split at `,`, `&`, `/` and at the words `and`, `und`, `et`, `y`, `e`, `en`, `i`) is the user's (`AppSettings.isMe`).
- **Field** `todoSuggestionsRaw: String?` on the note: nil = no suggestions offered; otherwise a JSON object with sorted keys, task key → `"taken"` or `"dismissed"`, `{}` when nothing was decided yet. Writing a summary sets it to `{}` when it is nil (states already there stay); "Suggest To-dos" removes the `"dismissed"` entries (and sets `{}` when nil). Unknown values are kept.
- **Waiting suggestions** (`pendingTodoSuggestions`): only when the field is set, the note has a summary and is no saved page, no to-do and not deleted: the tasks whose key has no state (exact or same task) and that match no title of a to-do made from the note.
- **iCloud:** `todoSuggestionsRaw` is stored on the note only when set. Both devices changed: the newer change wins, as for every field.
- **Test data:** debug builds read the launch argument `-seedMeetingWithTasks <title>` and add a summarized meeting with three tasks in a new bowl of that name (`TestSeed`).

## The to-dos folder

A virtual folder of every open to-do, sorted by due day. It is a view, not a bowl: nothing is moved or stored for it, it cannot be renamed, deleted or chosen as a place to file into, and the to-dos stay where they are (to-dos in no bowl also stay in the Inbox list).

- **Scope:** all open to-dos, from the Inbox and every bowl. One place for what is due is the point of it; a folder of the Inbox's to-dos alone would leave out everything filed. (The alternative, only to-dos in no bowl, was left out for that reason.)
- **Where:** the To-dos side of the Inbox's switch New · To-dos (owner revision 9; before, a page reached from a card at the top of the Inbox), "To-dos 5" (no count when none is open). Also the Go To row "Open To-dos" of Search & Ask, the hint about overdue to-dos, View › Open To-dos and the command bar on the Mac, the quick action and widgets, and `porridge://todos`: all open the Inbox on its To-dos side (Mac: the page `.todos`, the Inbox's row selected in the sidebar).
- **The side:** the Inbox's bar and head (title "Inbox"). Sections by due day, exactly the groups of the search chip "Open To-dos" (`Todos.dueGroups`): Overdue (header red), Today, Upcoming, No Due Date (headers green), each with its count, each sorted by due day; empty groups left out. Then "Done Today" (header navy): to-dos done since the start of today, the latest first. Rows are the ordinary rows (box, due day, progress, swipes and long press with the due date menu), and they name their bowls in the footer (`folder` symbol, violet, names joined by ", ", cut short first on a narrow row) in place of the date of the last change. Empty: "Nothing to Do", "Open to-dos from the inbox and every bowl show up here."
- **Plus:** the side's own green "+" in the Inbox's bar (iPhone) makes a new to-do at once (no menu), in no bowl; the "+" of the tab bar opens the Add sheet as everywhere.
- **Rules** (`Todos.folder`): open = to-do, not done, not deleted. Done today = to-do, done, not deleted, `todoDoneAt` not before the start of today. Overdue count = open ones whose due day is before today (it changes at midnight). Sorting and groups as in "Due day rules" above.
- **Test data:** debug builds read `-seedTodos <prefix>`: four open to-dos "<prefix> long overdue" (due 2000-01-01, in a new bowl named <prefix>), "<prefix> today", "<prefix> upcoming" (in five days), "<prefix> no date". They are made again on every launch; what an earlier launch made (to-dos of these names, the bowl when nothing else is in it) goes first, so a full run of the UI tests never sees two of them.

## Drag and drop on the Mac

Files, pictures, web links and text dragged from other apps onto the main window come in through the existing import (`PorridgeMac/Support/Intake.swift` → `ShareImport.add`, `NoteStore.importAudio`, `LinkImport.add`); there is no import logic of its own.

- **Where it lands:** a bowl in the sidebar takes it into that bowl (the row lights up). Anywhere else in the sidebar the Inbox row lights up. The list and the detail column (`ImportDropView`, root of both columns) put it into the open bowl, or into the Inbox when no bowl is open (to-dos, chat, archive, trash, search). The first new entry opens; when it would not show on the open page, the window goes to its bowl or the Inbox.
- **What it becomes:** each file is one entry. Audio and video become a recording that is transcribed. Pictures, PDFs and every other file become a file whose text is read (the same as Import File). A web address becomes a link. Text becomes a note. Nothing becomes a to-do, and links never make tasks.
- **Highlight:** while dragging over the list or the detail, the column gets a rounded accent frame and a card such as "Add 3 files to Inbox". Folders and packages (apps, bundles) are not imported: the card names them, and after the drop a notice says so. If only folders are dragged, the frame turns red with "Can't Import" and the drop is refused. Files that can't be copied are also named in a notice.
- **Editor and chat field:** text and links dropped onto the open note's text, or onto the chat field, are put in where they are dropped. Files and pictures dropped there go to the column's import instead (`NotePlatformTextView`, `ChatInputTextView`).
- **Sandbox:** dropped files are copied into the app's storage during the drop, because a sandboxed app can read them only then.
- **Check:** debug builds have `drop list|detail|editor <path>|<path>…` and `drop … text:<words>` in the debug driver. They run a drop through the column's own drop methods; `hover` shows the highlight without dropping.

## Chat data

The chat answers from one index of every living entry (`NoteIndex`, file `Application Support/Porridge/note-index.plist`). It is derived data: never synced, rebuilt from the notes at any time (deleting the file is safe). Code: `Porridge/Services/ChatDocument.swift` (entry, filters, context), `ChunkRanking.swift` (passages, BM25, vectors), `ChatService.swift` (passages of a note, prompt, sources), `NoteIndex.swift` (storage).

- **What is in it:** per note that is not in Recently Deleted one entry (`ChatDocument`) and its passages (`ChatChunk`). Kept per note with a hash (`Note.indexHash`) over title, text, summary, page text, URL, labels, created date, transcript, speakers, attachments, channel (`sourceRaw`), bowls (`projectIDs`), the legacy `archivedAt` (no longer used for notes, kept so cleared marks get re-indexed once) and the to-do fields (state, due day, done date, overdue); a note is cut again only when its hash changes. The index is refreshed before every question and 0.8 s after the note list changes (also after sync, which sets `updatedAt`), so creating, editing, filing, deleting and incoming sync all reach it.
- **Entry (`ChatDocument`):** `noteID`, `kind` (`todo` > `link` > `image` > `file` > `meeting` > `note`, the order of `Note.kindName`), `title` (`displayTitle`), `createdAt`, `bucketIDs` (`projectIDs`; empty = Inbox), `url`, `domain`, `todoState` ("open"/"done", nil otherwise), `dueDay` ("yyyy-MM-dd"), `doneAt`, `subtasksDone`/`subtasksTotal`, `tags`, `snippet` (first plain lines without headings of summary, else text (without its first line when that is the title), else page text, else the first 20 transcript segments; at most 200 characters, cut with "…"). Bowl names are not stored: they are looked up per question (bowls get renamed).
- **Header** of an entry, the same for its list line and every passage of it: `«Title» — <Kind>[ (domain) for links][, open|done[ on <EEE d MMM yyyy>][, due <d MMMM yyyy>[, overdue]][, n of m subtasks done]] · created <EEE d MMM yyyy> · Bowl: A | Bowls: A, B | Inbox[ · #tag #tag][ · <url>]`. Dates in English (`en_US_POSIX`), e.g. `Tue 17 Nov 2026`.
- **Passages (`ChatChunk`, field `part`):** `link` ("Saved link (domain): url"), `files` ("Image: …"/"File: …" names), `todo` (`todoLine`), `summary` ("Summary: …"), `text`, `page` ("Article (domain): …", "Text in the image: …", "Text of the file: …"), `transcript` ("Transcript: Speaker: …"), and `title` when there is nothing else. Cut at paragraph breaks into about 700 characters. Old files without `part` read it as `text`.
- **Question → filters (`ChatQuery.parse`):** words are the lowercased question split at everything but letters, digits and "-". Kind words (German and English, list in `ChatQuery.kindWords`): to-do (`todo(s)`, `to-do(s)`, `aufgabe(n)`, `task(s)`), link (`link(s)`, `lesezeichen`, `bookmark(s)`, `artikel`, `article(s)`, `webseite(n)`, `website(s)`), meeting (`meeting(s)`, `besprechung(en)`, `aufnahme(n)`, `recording(s)`, `transkript(e)`, `transcript(s)`, `call(s)`, `telefonat(e)`), image (`bild(er)`, `foto(s)`, `image(s)`, `photo(s)`, `screenshot(s)`), file (`datei(en)`, `file(s)`, `pdf(s)`, `dokument(e)`, `document(s)`); "note"/"Notiz" is no filter. State: open (`offen…`, `open`, `unerledigt…`, `ausstehend…`, `pending`, `anstehend…`), done (`erledigt…`, `done`, `completed`, `finished`, `abgehakt…`, `abgeschlossen…`, `geschafft`), overdue (`überfällig…`, `overdue`, `verspätet`, `late`). Due (`fällig…`, `due`, `deadline(s)`, `frist(en)`) makes the time about the due day. A bowl is named by its whole name as consecutive words; `inbox` means no bowl; `bowl(s)` (and the old `bucket(s)`) is dropped. Time (`ChatQuery.timeRange`, first that fits, days `[start, end)` in the device calendar, weeks from Monday): a written date (`17.11.`, `17.11.2026`, `2026-11-17`); `[letzten|nächsten] N Tage|Wochen|Monate` (N digits or two…ten; back: `[today−N, tomorrow)`, ahead: `[today, today+N+1)`, a month counts 30 days); übermorgen, vorgestern, heute/today, gestern/yesterday, morgen/tomorrow; nächste/letzte/diese Woche; nächsten/letzten/diesen Monat; vague recent words (`letzten Tage`, `kürzlich`, `recently`, …) = the last 7 days and today; a weekday name = the last such day (today included), or the next one (today included) when the question is about a due day or open to-dos. Then: a due day or "overdue" adds kind to-do; a state without a kind means to-do; a question only about to-dos without a state lists open ones; done with a time is about the done date. Every word used for a filter is left out of the words searched for; the rest goes through the stop words (`ChatRetrieval.stopWords`).
- **Match (`ChatQuery.matches`):** kind in the kinds asked (any when none); in one of the bowls asked or, with `inbox`, in none; the state only applies to to-dos (overdue = open and due before today); the time is checked on the due day (`due`), the done date (`done`), or by default on the due day of a to-do with one, else the created date.
- **Context (`ChatRetrieval.context`):** without a filter, the best passages of all entries (`select`: BM25 plus vectors, at most 10 passages, 6500 characters, 3 per note; a named time first; the newest when no word fits; when the question had words to search for and none fits, the context is marked `wordsNotFound` and the prompt says "No entry contains the words of the question. The newest entries, only in case they answer it:" instead of "Excerpts:"). With a filter: (1) every matching entry as a list line (header and snippet), up to 40; open to-dos by due day (undated last), then done ones by done date, the rest newest first; to-dos first when the question is about them. When words are left to search for, entries that have them come first (most words first) and their line ends with `— mentions: w1, w2`. (2) The content passages (summary, text, page, transcript) of the matches that fit the words. (3) Further content passages (summary, text, page, transcript) of the first 8 matches in order, 12 per entry when there are at most 2 matches, 4 for at most 5, else 2, up to 6500 characters. (4) When words were left but no match has them, the best passages of the other entries (3000 characters), marked as outside. When nothing matches the filters at all, the model is told so and gets the best passages of all entries, marked as outside.
- **Prompt:** the system names today (`Today is <complete date> (EEE d MMM yyyy).`), says to answer only from the items and not to guess, to answer list questions in full from the list of matches and add nothing (for a topic, to take the entries that are about it), to write the answer in words (every entry by its title with the facts asked for, never numbers alone) with the numbers used after the facts like `[2]`, to answer in the language of the question, and to say plainly that nothing was found when the items do not hold the answer. The user part: the last 4 messages (500 characters each), then `Matching entries (<filters in words>): all N entries.` (or `the first M of N entries.` when cut) with `[n] <header>` and the snippet indented on the next line, or `No entry matches (<filters>).`; then `Excerpts from the matching entries:` (`Excerpts:` without filters) with `[n] <header>` and the passage; then `Excerpts from other entries (…)`; then `Question: …`. Numbers run through list lines, passages and outside passages in that order. The Apple model gets at most 7000 characters, half of them for the list.
- **Sources:** the notes behind the numbers the answer quotes. With no number, the first three passages that are neither list lines nor outside; when there are none, the first three list lines. Never outside passages, and none at all when the context is `wordsNotFound` (an answer "nothing found" has no sources).
- **Test data:** debug builds started with the environment variable `PORRIDGE_TEST_DATA=<folder>` keep the store (`test.store`), recordings, files and this index in that folder and never sync (`DataLocation`). On the Mac the debug driver's `seed-chat` adds the 15 entries of `ChatDataTests` there (`TestSeed.seedChat`); `ask <question>`, `new-chat`, `chat-dump` and `chat-source <n>` try the chat.

## Summaries of incoming entries

What comes in from outside gets a summary by itself, in the summary field it always had, as soon as its content is there; the user's own notes, to-dos and recordings are summarized only when asked (they get templates and suggested to-dos). Code: `Porridge/Services/AutoSummary.swift` (rules), `ProcessingService` (job `.autoSummary`, `summarizeIncoming`), `SummaryService.summarize(onlyIfEmpty:)`, `SummaryService.removeSummary`.

- **Setting:** "Summarize Incoming Entries" (Settings, AI Summary; key `summarizeIncoming`, default on). Off: nothing is summarized by itself. Nothing runs without a model that is ready (`SummaryService.canRunWithoutAsking`: an installed on-device model or a cloud model with its key); nothing is downloaded for it.
- **Incoming kinds (`AutoSummary.kind`), in this order:** a to-do never; a link (`url` set); an image or a file (`isStored`: attachments, channel share, no URL, no recording); imported audio (a recording whose channel is `import` or `plaud`); shared text (channel share, no attachments). Everything else (own notes, own meetings, a note that got a file attached) is not incoming.
- **Material:** the page text of a link, the text read out of an image or file (`pageText`), the text of shared text, the transcript of imported audio. Trimmed; at least 280 characters, or nothing happens (a photo without text, a page that could not be read).
- **Due (`AutoSummary.isDue`):** setting on; not in Recently Deleted; `summary` is nil and `summarizedAt` is nil; an incoming kind; status ready (page loaded, text read, transcribed) and no part waiting to be transcribed; enough material; and either the entry came in on this device, or it was last changed at least 2 hours ago and created at most 30 days ago (catching up for another device).
- **Which device:** the device an entry comes in on marks it (`AutoSummary.markOrigin`, a list of note ids in this device's settings, key `autoSummaryOrigins`, never synced): share sheet and share inbox, pasted, dropped or added links, picked or dropped files, shared text, imported audio, Plaud. That device summarizes as soon as the content is there. Another device leaves it alone until 2 hours passed without a change and there is still no summary (e.g. no model on the first device). Nothing new is synced: the summary, `summaryModel` and `summarizedAt` travel as they always did. When two devices still both write one, the sync keeps one copy as with any other change. The mark is removed after the summary is written and when the note is gone or has a summary (at launch).
- **Never twice, never over the user:** only an entry with neither `summary` nor `summarizedAt` is summarized; checked when queued, when the job starts and again after the model answered (`onlyIfEmpty`; nothing is written when a summary was written, edited or deleted meanwhile). Deleting a summary (or emptying it in the editor) keeps `summarizedAt` and sets `updatedAt` (`removeSummary`): a written summary that was deleted never comes back by itself, on any device. An editor opened and closed on no summary leaves `summarizedAt` nil.
- **When it runs:** right after the content arrived (page loaded, attachments read, transcript done), at launch, when the app comes to the front (iOS), when the notes change (new entries, iCloud changes; after the index refresh), and every 15 minutes while the app runs. One job per entry in the existing serial queue; the row shows the stage "Summarizing…" like any job. Errors are quiet: an entry that failed is tried again after 15 minutes, at most 3 times per launch, and again at the next launch.
- **No tasks:** links, images, files and shared text are summarized with the page prompt, which asks for no tasks, and get no suggested to-dos (`TodoSuggestions.offer` skips saved entries). Imported audio is summarized like a meeting with the General template; its tasks are only offered, never made. A summary of an entry in the Inbox may suggest a bowl, as before.
- **Chat:** a new summary changes the note's index hash, so the chat index cuts the entry again; its `ChatDocument.snippet` starts with the summary (headings left out).

## Meetings from the calendar

The calendar is read, never written: an appointment only starts or prepares a Record Meeting. No editing, no answering invitations, no reminders, no tasks from appointments.

- **Which appointments (`MeetingAlerts.qualifies`):** at a time of day (not all-day), not declined by the user, in a calendar that counts, with somebody else invited (organizer or attendee, rooms and resources left out) or a video link (Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex, Whereby, GoTo, FaceTime, Chime, Jitsi, Around, BlueJeans; found in the URL, place or notes).
- **Setting:** "Alert Before Meetings" in Settings › Recording on both apps (key `meetingAlerts`, this device only). Settled once when this version first runs: on when calendar access was already given, off otherwise. Turning it on asks for the calendar (and on iOS for notifications). Each calendar can be switched off (`meetingAlertsExcludedCalendars`; new calendars count).
- **Mac:** a small floating panel in the top right corner about a minute before the start (until two minutes after; once per appointment and start), on every Space, without taking the focus: "Meeting at 10:00", the title, "With Anna, Tom and 2 more", Record (filled sky), Join (only with a video link), and a close button. It leaves by itself five minutes after the start. Not while a meeting is being recorded. Record starts Record Meeting for the appointment in the front window; Join opens the link. The status item shows the minutes to the next such meeting ("12m") from half an hour before, and its menu has "Next Meeting · 10:00" with Record Meeting and Join (checkbox "Show Countdown in Menu Bar", key `meetingCountdown`, default on).
- **iOS:** a local notification a minute before, planned for the next 36 hours whenever the app runs or a calendar changes (at most 20): the title, "Starts at 10:00 · with Anna, Tom", actions Record (opens the app and starts Record Meeting for the appointment) and Join (video calls only).
- **The appointment of a meeting (`MeetingLinks`):** a meeting started for an appointment (sheet, alert) keeps it on this device (key `meetingAppointments`, 180 days). Otherwise, and on other devices, the appointment with the meeting's title that overlaps the recording is looked up in the calendar. Nothing new is synced.
- **Naming speakers:** on a meeting with a known appointment, a speaker pill opens a menu "Invited" with the people not yet given to another speaker, then the user's name, then "Other Name…". A chosen name teaches the voice like a typed one (`VoiceProfiles`). The people also go into the summary's header ("Invited: …").
- **Today (Up Next):** the day's appointments (declined ones left out), each with the entries linked to it, then the open to-dos due. Today shows only what is open: done to-dos never show there, on today or any other day, also not under an appointment; a to-do ticked in Today leaves the list with the undo toast ("Done: “…”", Undo puts back the state and the subtasks). It stays where it is (its bowl, the Inbox) and in Open To-dos under "Done Today". It pages a day at a time (`TodayDay`); the day is view state only, not kept: it starts on today at launch, when Today is opened again and when the day turns, and the Today card on home and the sidebar count always show today. The title names the day: "Today", "Tomorrow", "Yesterday", otherwise weekday and date (template `EEEEdMMM`, with `y` in another year), in the device's locale. To-dos (`TodayTodos`): on today the open ones due today or before (late ones too); on another day only the open ones due on exactly that day. Appointments of other days are read from the calendar when shown and again when it changes; only today's are kept. **iOS:** the bar's title is the day, and a previous-day and a next-day chevron sit together in one pill at the bar's right (no row of its own, no swipe: the list rows keep theirs); on another day a tap on the title goes back to today. **Mac:** the header's title is the day (full date as tooltip), with ‹, "Today" (only active on another day) and › at its right; with the list focused ← and → page and T goes back to today.
- **Summary after renames (`SummaryRefresh`):** the new name replaces the old one as a whole word in the summary right away. Six seconds after the last rename the summary is written again when the meeting has one, "Summarize Incoming Entries" is on, the model of the last summary (else the default) can run without a download, and the summary is as the model wrote it (no ticked task, not edited since; known on the device that wrote it). Same template as last chosen on this device, else General.
- **Default meeting summary:** Summary, Key Points, Decisions (always there), Action Items with an owner for each task (the person who took it on, as the transcript names them). Team Meeting and Customer Call have Decisions too; custom templates keep their sections.

## Chat model

The chat can answer with another model than the summaries. Code: `SummaryModel.chat` / `chatChoice` / `resolveChat`, `ChatBudget` (`ChatDocument.swift`), `ChatService.message(for:model:)`, `CloudLLM.error(status:message:provider:)`.

- **Setting "Chat Model"** (Settings, AI Summary, under "Default Model"; Mac: AI pane): "Same as Default" (the default, nothing stored) or one model of the same list as the default model (on-device models that are there, then the added cloud models). Stored on this device only, key `chatModel`, value the model's id (`local:<LocalModel>` or `cloud:<AIProvider>:<model id>`), like the default model it is not synced. Summaries always use the default model.
- **Which model answers (`resolveChat`):** the stored model when it is in the list and can run now (`isUsable`: an on-device model that is installed; a cloud model whose provider has a key, or for the custom server an address); otherwise, quietly, the default model. A model that is deleted, removed or loses its key therefore falls back without a message; the stored id stays, so it comes back when the model does.
- **In the chat:** a pill on the right of the row on top (iOS: menu with "Same as Default (<default name>)" and every model as "<name> · <place>"; Mac: a small pop-up button with the same items) shows the model that answers (cloud icon or chip icon, its name) and sets the same choice as the setting.
- **Privacy:** with a cloud model, the setting shows under the menu "The question and excerpts of your notes goes to <provider>." ("your server" for the custom one), worded like the summary sheets; on the Mac the same text is the hint row and the tooltip of the chat's model button. Nothing is shown for on-device models.
- **Room per model (`ChatBudget`):** Apple model: list 40 entries, filled passages 6500 characters, outside passages 3000, passages without filters 6500 characters and at most 10, prompt cut at 7000 characters (list at most half). Other on-device models: the same without a cut. Cloud models: list 100, filled 20000, outside 8000, without filters 20000 characters and at most 30 passages, prompt cut at 100000 characters.
- **Errors in the chat** (shown as an error answer): no key "Please add your <provider> API key in Settings."; HTTP 401/403 "<provider> did not accept the API key. Check it in Settings, under AI."; 429 "<provider> is busy or your usage limit is reached. Try again later."; 5xx "<provider> has a problem right now (<status>). Try again later."; other HTTP errors "<provider>: <message of the service>"; no connection "Could not reach <place>. Check the internet connection and try again."; timeout "<place> took too long to answer. Try again in a moment." (custom server: "Your server" / "your server"). Summaries get the same HTTP messages. No streaming: the answer comes whole, as before.

## Chat actions

The chat can change entries, never on its own: the model proposes, the user confirms. Code: `Porridge/Services/ChatActions.swift` (calls, tools, ACTION parser, checks, runner, store), `EntryActions.swift` (the changes and their undo, reusable outside the chat), cards in `Views/Chat/ChatActionViews.swift` and `PorridgeMac/Chat/ChatActionCardsView.swift`.

- **Actions:** `move_to_bowl` (one bowl at most, "Inbox" takes it out; an unknown name makes a new bowl), `create_bowl`, `set_todo` (open/done, due day; never for links, saved files, meetings), `set_tags` (the tag field; `#words` in the text stay), `set_document_info` (kind, sender, date, amount through `Documents.set`, so it counts as an edit by hand), `create_note` (note or to-do, optionally in a bowl). Nothing deletes or archives.
- **Proposing:** cloud models get the actions as native tools (OpenAI-style `tools`, Anthropic `tools`, Gemini `functionDeclarations`); a provider that refuses tools (HTTP 400/404/405/422/501) is asked again with ACTION lines. On-device models (MLX, Apple) write `ACTION: {"action": …}` lines (also `ACTION name key=value` and fenced ```action blocks), and are told about them only when the question asks for a change (`ChatActionTools.asksForChange`). The lines are taken out of the shown answer.
- **Checking (`ChatActionResolver`):** an entry is named by its item number (this answer's items, then "Entries named earlier in this chat", the sources and action targets of earlier answers), or by title among those; an entry outside them only when the question names its title. Several fits, or several bowls of one name, become a question back; no-ops, links as to-dos, archived bowls and unknown actions become remarks under the answer. At most 10 per answer.
- **Confirming:** each action is a card under the answer with Confirm / Cancel, "Confirm All (n)" for several. Made changes show a navy toast with Undo for 8 s (`ChatUndoToast`); undo restores the touched fields and removes what was made (a bowl only while empty). Card states are kept on this device in UserDefaults (`chatActions`, newest 80 answers), not in the store.
