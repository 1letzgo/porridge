# Porridge design

The look follows Things 3: quiet white pages, big bold titles, thin lines, and color that says what something is. This file replaces the direction chosen in `design-demos/direction-approved.md` where the app has moved on. iOS, Mac and Android follow the same rules; colors and sizes live in `Porridge/Design/Theme.swift` and `android/.../ui/Theme.kt`.

## Color

| Color | Value | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sky | `#24B4E9`, text `#0B79A8` | The plus button, selection, toggles, the mic button, notes and their date headers |
| Navy | `#1F3667`, dark `#B0C4EC` | Titles of notes, icons on bars and buttons, symbols on sky, orange and green, meetings, Archive, pins |
| Green | `#29BD80`, text `#0F8A5A` | To-dos and their box, check boxes in summaries |
| Violet | `#7C5CD6`, dark `#9B82E8` | Buckets (projects in the code) |
| Orange | `#FA8F24`, text `#B55D00` | Saved links |
| Red | System red, text `#D93025`, dark `#FF6B60` | Recently Deleted, everything that deletes, recording (the stop button and the mic while it runs), and the due day of an open to-do that is late |

Speakers have their own sequence of colors (`speakerColors`), starting with sky and orange. It says who is talking and nothing about the kind of thing.

Every kind of thing keeps its color everywhere: in the list icon, its section header and its page.

Sky, orange and green are for surfaces. As text or a symbol on a white page they use the darker "text" value, which reaches 4.5:1 (green 3:1, used only bold). Symbols on a full fill of them are navy, never white. In dark mode the bright values are the readable ones. The Apple platforms also have a variant for Increase Contrast.

## Layout

- **Pages** are white (near black in dark mode) with hairlines as separators. Note pages scroll as a whole, head included, on iPhone and Android. The Mac keeps the head of a note in place.
- **Moving between pages:** on iPhone, iPad and Android pages slide in and out as the system does it, and going back follows the finger where the platform offers that. The Mac sidebar changes in place.
- **Section headers** are small and bold in the color of their kind, with a line below. Archive and Recently Deleted are tappable headers with a chevron at the bottom of the home list.
- **Bottom bar:** chat and search as white round buttons with navy icons on the left, the blue plus on the right. Each opens a page of its own: the search with its field on top, the chat with its field at the bottom.
- **Chat:** a page of its own that asks the buckets, with its field at the bottom instead of the round buttons. A chat is about all buckets or one (a pill on top says which; a new chat can choose), answers name the notes they come from as pills, and chats are kept: the bar of the page has the list of chats and a new chat.
- **Plus menu:** what can be created (Bucket, Link, Meeting, To-do, Note), each in the color of its kind.
- **Import menu:** a button in the bar beside Settings, on the home list and in a bucket: Import Audio, Import Plaud once signed in, Photo or File.
- **Live Activity and notification:** the running meeting with its clock, pause and stop. Navy on the light Lock Screen card, white on the black Dynamic Island.
- **Inbox:** rows like in every list. A bucket is chosen in the long press of a row; only a suggested bucket shows as a pill under it.
- **Home list:** the section "Up Next" with the Inbox and, below it, the to-dos folder while there are to-dos to show; then the section "Buckets": the pinned ones and the 5 latest others; "More" unfolds the rest in place.
- **Row icons:** a rounded square as high as the two lines of the row, in the color of the kind, with the kind's symbol (`Note.kindSymbol`): to-do `checklist` (green), meeting `waveform` (navy), note `text.alignleft` or `paperclip` with a file (sky), link `link` (orange), image `photo`, PDF `doc.richtext`, file `doc` (navy). The same symbol names the kind in the plus menu, the bar of its page and the pills that point to a note (the source of a to-do, the sources of a chat answer). A pinned entry wears a small navy pin badge on the top right of the icon's border.
- **Kinds:** to-dos, meetings, notes, links, images and files. Images and files are two kinds that behave alike. A bucket's filter pills show only the kinds it holds.
- **Check boxes:** green squares for to-dos and for tasks inside summaries. Circles only for checklists you write in a note (the subtasks of a to-do are such a checklist).

## Controls

- **Sheets:** "Cancel" on the left when closing throws work away, "Done" on the right when nothing is lost.
- **Deleting for good always asks first.** Moving something to Recently Deleted does not, because it can come back.
- **Main buttons** are a wash of their color with a navy label. Disabled buttons fade; destructive ones turn red.
- **Summary models** are one list, on-device and cloud alike, with one default. Adding one happens in a sheet; a meeting chooses its model and template in one sheet, and that choice holds for this summary only.
- **Menus** are grouped by a line: the note and its summary, the recording, what deletes.
- **Native controls first.** Custom ones only where the system has no equivalent.
- **Wording:** English Title Case for buttons and titles, "…" without a space before it.

## To-dos

A to-do is a note like any other, not a list of its own: it lives in the timeline, the Inbox, buckets, the archive, Recently Deleted, the search, the chat, the sync and the export the same way. What makes it a to-do is a done state; its subtasks are the checklist lines of its text. (The to-dos removed in 1.1 were items of their own with a tab of their own; that parallel world does not come back.)

**Using them**

- **Creating:** "To-do" in the plus menu makes a new note that is a to-do, the same way as "Note" (in a bucket it goes into it). Any note that is no meeting and no saved link or file can be turned into a to-do and back: "Convert to To-do" / "Convert to Note" in the note's More menu and in the long press of its row. The text stays as it is.
- **Icon and box:** a to-do has the icon of its kind like every entry (green rounded square, `checklist`; Material `checklist` on Android), the same whether it is open, done or late: the state is the box's and the due day's, not the icon's. The box that ticks it is a separate control at the end of the row, 26 pt drawn and 44 pt to tap: open a green outline with a light green wash, done filled green with a navy `checkmark`. On its page the same box stands before the title. Icon and box are apart because the icon of every other kind opens the entry rather than changing it, and an empty box at the start of a row read as a missing icon.
- **Row:** tapping the box ticks the to-do right in the list. A done one is struck through and grey. The row's preview shows the open subtasks (joined by " · "), its footer the progress "2/5" with a `checkmark.circle` symbol, like the circles of the subtasks (green once all are done). Swipe from the left and the long press have "Mark as Done" / "Mark as Not Done".
- **Page:** the box stands before the title; the bar says "To-do" in green. The meta line adds "Done …" and "2 of 5 done". The body is the normal editor; its circles are the subtasks: tap to tick, Return for the next, Tab to indent, the format menu moves a line up or down ("Move Line Up/Down", ⌥⌘↑/↓ on a keyboard), deleting the line deletes the subtask. "Add Subtask" below the text adds an empty one at the end with the cursor in it.
- **Done:** a done to-do stays where it is, ticked and grey, for the rest of the day. From the next day on it counts as archived: out of the Inbox and the buckets, in the Archive and the search. Taking it out of the archive ("Mark as Not Done") opens it again. Archiving by hand works as for every note.
- **Due day:** a to-do can be due on a day (no time of day). Its page has a chip first in the row of buckets and tags: "+ Due Date" while there is none, otherwise the day ("Today", "Fri", "12 Dec") in green, or in red when it is late. The chip is a menu: Today, Tomorrow, Next Week (the first day of next week, as the calendar counts weeks), Pick Date… (the system's calendar in a sheet), and Clear Due Date. The long press of a list row has the same menu ("Due Date"). A new to-do sets it the same way, right on its page. In the row the day stands in the footer with a calendar symbol: red and semibold when an open to-do is late (from midnight after the day, with a calendar symbol with an exclamation mark), green and semibold when it is due today, grey otherwise; a done to-do is never marked. "Make To-do" from a summary sets no day. There are no reminders or notifications and nothing goes to a calendar.
- **Filter:** the search has the chip "Open To-dos" (to-dos not done and not archived), grouped by due day: Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No Due Date (empty groups left out), each by due day. A bucket has the filter pill "To-dos" and the section "To-dos" first: open ones by due day (earliest first, those without one after them), then those done today; its row counts "Open To-dos". Everywhere else the timeline stays as it is.
- **From a meeting (suggested to-dos):** see the section below. The single "Make To-do" button on each task line is gone: the suggestions replace it. A task line that became a to-do wears the to-do symbol (`checklist`, green) at its end.

**Format** (iOS is the source; Android mirrors it exactly)

- Three fields of `Note` (Room: `NoteEntity`), all optional, nil/absent by default:
  - `todoStateRaw: String?` — `"open"`, `"done"`, or `"none"` for a to-do turned back into a note. Absent (nil) = the note never was a to-do. `isTodo` = open or done; `isDone` = done.
  - `todoDoneAt: Date?` — when it was ticked; nil while open.
  - `todoSourceID: String?` — the `id` (uppercase UUID) of the note whose summary task it came from.
  - `todoDueDay: String?` — the due day as `"yyyy-MM-dd"` (zero-padded, Gregorian): a day of the calendar, no time, no time zone, so it is the same day on every device wherever it is. `"none"` = a due day was taken away. Nil = never set. It counts only while the note is a to-do (`todoDue`); a note turned back keeps it stored and does not show it. Anything that is not ten characters of three numbers counts as no due day.
- **Sync record** (`notes` in the sync file): the same names. `todoStateRaw` string, `todoDoneAt` milliseconds since 1970, `todoSourceID` string. A field is left out when nil, so the records of all other notes (and their hashes) stay as they were.
- **Applying a record:** only when it has `todoStateRaw` are the three fields `todoStateRaw`, `todoDoneAt`, `todoSourceID` taken from it (missing `todoDoneAt`/`todoSourceID` then mean nil). `todoDueDay` on its own: taken when the record has it (a string, `"none"` included), kept as it is when it has not. So clearing writes `"none"` (only when there was a value; clearing a never set day writes nothing), and a version that knows no due days cannot lose or erase one. When both devices changed the note, the newer record wins as for every field. A record without `todoStateRaw` comes from a version that knows no to-dos (or the note never was one): the device keeps its own three fields. That is why turning a to-do back writes `"none"` instead of removing the field.
- **Subtasks:** lines matching `^([ \t]*)[-*+][ \t]+\[([ xX])\](?:[ \t]+(.*))?$` (the editor's checklist syntax), outside ```` ``` ```` code blocks, with non-empty text after trimming. Ticked = `x` or `X`. Indent = (spaces + 2 per tab) / 2. The app writes `- [ ] ` and `- [x] `, two spaces per indent level. Progress = ticked / all; "complete" needs at least one subtask.
- **Done rules** (on a change of the subtasks from old to new text, `Todos.stateAfterEdit`): open and the new text complete while the old was not → done (`todoDoneAt` = now). Done and the new text has an open subtask while the old had none, or fewer are ticked than before → open (`todoDoneAt` = nil). Nothing else changes the state: reopening a to-do by hand leaves its subtasks ticked and it stays open. Ticking the to-do itself ticks all its subtasks; unticking it leaves them.
- **Due day rules:** today = the device's current day in its time zone, as `"yyyy-MM-dd"`. Keys compare as strings. Overdue = open to-do and due day < today. Due today = due day == today. Labels (`Todos.dueLabel`): days from today 0 "Today", 1 "Tomorrow", -1 "Yesterday", 2 to 6 the abbreviated weekday (template `EEE`), otherwise day and abbreviated month (template `dMMM`) in this year, with the year (template `dMMMy`) in another; formatted in the device's locale. Sorting (`Todos.byDue`): open to-dos by due day ascending, those without a due day after them in their previous order, then the done ones. Groups (`Todos.dueGroups`): Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No Due Date.
- **Put away:** `isArchived` = `archivedAt != nil` or (done and `todoDoneAt` before the start of today, local time). `isInInbox` uses `isArchived`. "Unarchive" of such a to-do sets it open (and `archivedAt` nil).
- **Merge of ticks** (`SyncMerge`, `Todos.mergeRecords`): the sync base of a to-do record also keeps its `text` as last written to the file (`SyncBase.text`, only for records whose `todoStateRaw` is open or done). When both sides changed a to-do, the newer record wins as for every note, but the subtasks the other side ticked or unticked since the base text are applied to it, as long as the newer one left that subtask alone. Subtasks are matched by their lowercased text and how many lines with the same text came before. If that changed the newer text, the done rules above run on it (from the newer text to the merged one, done time = the merge time) and the result is applied on this device and written to the file. Without a base text the newer record wins unchanged.
- **Chat:** a to-do adds one passage to the chat index before its summary and text: `To-do, open: <title>` or `To-do, done on <day month year>: <title>`; with a due day `, due <d MMMM yyyy in English, e.g. 12 December 2026>` follows the state, and `, overdue` after it when it is open and late; then `, <n> of <m> subtasks done` when it has subtasks, then the colon and the title (`ChatRetrieval.todoLine`). The passage is dated by the start of its due day (other passages by the note's creation), so a question about a time ("this week") finds what is due then. The index hash of a to-do includes `todoStateRaw`, `todoDueDay`, `todoDoneAt` and whether it is overdue, so the passage is cut again when one of them changes (also at midnight).
- **Export:** `exportText` adds the line `[ ] To-do` or `[x] To-do, done` after the head, with `, due <d MMMM yyyy>` when it has a due day.

## Suggested to-dos

The tasks of a summary are offered as to-dos; nothing becomes a to-do without a tap. Meetings and notes with a summary have them, saved links and files never (their summaries have no tasks).

**Using them**

- **Where:** after a summary is written, the summary tab of the page shows a card "Suggested To-dos" first, above the summary: a green wash with a thin green border, the heading with `checklist` in green. Each open task is a line with a selection circle (`checkmark.circle.fill` green / `circle` grey), its title as an editable text field, and under it the owner (`person`) and the due day (`calendar`, same labels as everywhere) when the summary names them.
- **Picked at first:** the user's own tasks and those without an owner, when the app knows the user's name (Settings, "You", or the voice marked as the user's); all of them when it does not. Somebody else's task stays unpicked, because a meeting usually gives the user a few tasks and the others theirs.
- **Taking them on:** the button "Add 3 To-dos" (the count of picked ones; "Add 1 To-do"; disabled at 0) makes one open to-do per picked task: title = the (edited) task, in the same buckets as the meeting (`filedAt` set when there are any), `todoSourceID` = the meeting, `todoDueDay` = the named day, and text `Owner: <name>` when it is somebody else's task. Its long press has "Add as One To-do with Subtasks": one to-do "Follow-ups: <meeting title>" with the picked tasks as `- [ ] ` lines (`<task> (<owner>)` for somebody else's), due on the earliest named day. The tasks not picked are dismissed at the same time, so the card is done.
- **Dismiss** next to it dismisses all the tasks of the card.
- **Back again:** "Suggest To-dos" in the page's More menu (with a summary, not for a to-do or a saved page) brings the dismissed tasks back and shows the summary tab. Taken ones stay taken.
- **Made to-dos:** below the summary a list "To-dos" shows the to-dos made from the note (by `todoSourceID`), open by due day first, each with its box (ticked right there), title, due day and a chevron; a tap opens it. The to-do's page has the pill back to the meeting.
- **List row:** a note with waiting suggestions says "3 suggested to-dos" (`checklist`, green text) in its footer ("1 suggested to-do"), left out where the row is narrow.

**Format**

- The summary prompt asks for task lines as `- [ ] <task, a short instruction clear on its own> – <owner, if known> – <due date as YYYY-MM-DD, only if a date or deadline was named>`, owner and date left out when not named (a note's summary: `- [ ] <task> – <due date as YYYY-MM-DD, if named>`). No extra model call: the suggestions are read from the summary.
- **Tasks** (`TodoSuggestions.parse`): the open task lines of `SummaryTasks.parse` (ticked ones are not offered). The text is split at ` – ` or ` — ` (or ` - ` when there is neither): the first part is the title (`**` and leading or trailing dashes and colons removed), each further part trimmed of spaces, brackets, `*_.,;` and dashes is a due day if it reads as one, otherwise the owner (the first part without a digit), unless it is a placeholder (`-`, `?`, `n/a`, `none`, `unknown`, `tbd`, `not named`, `keine`, `niemand`, `unbekannt`, `offen`, `nicht genannt`, …, anything starting with `<` or containing "if known", "if named", "falls"). Each task once, by key.
- **Due day** (`TodoSuggestions.dueDay`, relative to the note's `createdAt` in the device's calendar): `yyyy-m-d`; `d.m.yyyy`, `d.m.yy` (20yy) or `d.m.`; a day and a month name (`28 November`, `28. Nov.`, `3 décembre`, `4 de diciembre de 2026`) or a month name and a day (`November 28, 2026`), month names and short forms in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese and Polish. Without a year: the first such day on or after the meeting's day. Invalid days and words without a date ("next Friday", "end of the month", "in 2 weeks") give none.
- **Key** (`TodoSuggestions.key`): the title case- and diacritic-folded, split at everything that is not a letter or digit, joined by single spaces ("Prüfe Ölstand!" → `prufe olstand`). **Same task** (`isSame`): equal keys, or both with three words or more and at least 60 % of the union of their words shared (Jaccard), so a summary written again keeps what was decided.
- **Mine:** no owner, or one of the owner's names (split at `,`, `&`, `/` and at the words `and`, `und`, `et`, `y`, `e`, `en`, `i`) is the user's (`AppSettings.isMe`).
- **Field** `todoSuggestionsRaw: String?` on the note: nil = no suggestions offered; otherwise a JSON object with sorted keys, task key → `"taken"` or `"dismissed"`, `{}` when nothing was decided yet. Writing a summary sets it to `{}` when it is nil (states already there stay); "Suggest To-dos" removes the `"dismissed"` entries (and sets `{}` when nil). Unknown values are kept.
- **Waiting suggestions** (`pendingTodoSuggestions`): only when the field is set, the note has a summary and is no saved page, no to-do and not deleted: the tasks whose key has no state (exact or same task) and that match no title of a to-do made from the note.
- **Sync:** `todoSuggestionsRaw` in the note's record, written only when set; a record without it keeps the device's value, one with it replaces it. Both devices changed: the newer record wins, as for every field.
- **Test data:** debug builds read the launch argument `-seedMeetingWithTasks <title>` and add a summarized meeting with three tasks in a new bucket of that name (`TestSeed`).

## The to-dos folder

A virtual folder of every open to-do, sorted by due day. It is a view, not a bucket: nothing is moved, stored or synced for it, it cannot be renamed, deleted or chosen as a place to file into, and the to-dos stay where they are (to-dos in no bucket also stay in the Inbox list).

- **Scope:** all open to-dos, from the Inbox and every bucket. One place for what is due is the point of it; a folder of the Inbox's to-dos alone would leave out everything filed. (The alternative, only to-dos in no bucket, was left out for that reason.)
- **Where:** on the home list, the second entry of the section "Up Next", right under "Inbox": the kind's icon (green rounded square, `checklist`), the title "To-dos", under it "5 open" ("1 open", "Nothing open" when only done ones are left), followed by " · 2 overdue" in red and semibold when some are late. It is left out when there is nothing to show (no open to-do and none done today). On the Mac it is the row under the Inbox in the sidebar.
- **The page:** title "To-dos" with the green `checklist` square in the bar. Sections by due day, exactly the groups of the search chip "Open To-dos" (`Todos.dueGroups`): Overdue (header red), Today, Upcoming, No Due Date (headers green), each with its count, each sorted by due day; empty groups left out. Then "Done Today" (header navy): to-dos done since the start of today, the latest first. Rows are the ordinary rows (box, due day, progress, swipes and long press with the due date menu), and they name their buckets in the footer (`folder` symbol, violet, names joined by ", ", cut short first on a narrow row) in place of the date of the last change. Empty: "Nothing to Do", "Open to-dos from the inbox and every bucket show up here."
- **Plus:** on this page the plus makes a new to-do at once (no menu), in no bucket.
- **Rules** (`Todos.folder`): open = to-do, not done, not deleted, `archivedAt` nil. Done today = to-do, done, not deleted, `archivedAt` nil, `todoDoneAt` not before the start of today. Overdue count = open ones whose due day is before today (it changes at midnight). Sorting and groups as in "Due day rules" above.
- **Test data:** debug builds read `-seedTodos <prefix>`: four open to-dos "<prefix> long overdue" (due 2000-01-01, in a new bucket named <prefix>), "<prefix> today", "<prefix> upcoming" (in five days), "<prefix> no date".

## Drag and drop on the Mac

Files, pictures, web links and text dragged from other apps onto the main window come in through the existing import (`PorridgeMac/Support/Intake.swift` → `ShareImport.add`, `NoteStore.importAudio`, `LinkImport.add`); there is no import logic of its own.

- **Where it lands:** a bucket in the sidebar takes it into that bucket (the row lights up). Anywhere else in the sidebar the Inbox row lights up. The list and the detail column (`ImportDropView`, root of both columns) put it into the open bucket, or into the Inbox when no bucket is open (to-dos, chat, archive, trash, search). The first new entry opens; when it would not show on the open page, the window goes to its bucket or the Inbox.
- **What it becomes:** each file is one entry. Audio and video become a recording that is transcribed. Pictures, PDFs and every other file become a file whose text is read (the same as Import File). A web address becomes a link. Text becomes a note. Nothing becomes a to-do, and links never make tasks.
- **Highlight:** while dragging over the list or the detail, the column gets a rounded accent frame and a card such as "Add 3 files to Inbox". Folders and packages (apps, bundles) are not imported: the card names them, and after the drop a notice says so. If only folders are dragged, the frame turns red with "Can't Import" and the drop is refused. Files that can't be copied are also named in a notice.
- **Editor and chat field:** text and links dropped onto the open note's text, or onto the chat field, are put in where they are dropped. Files and pictures dropped there go to the column's import instead (`NotePlatformTextView`, `ChatInputTextView`).
- **Sandbox:** dropped files are copied into the app's storage during the drop, because a sandboxed app can read them only then.
- **Check:** debug builds have `drop list|detail|editor <path>|<path>…` and `drop … text:<words>` in the debug driver. They run a drop through the column's own drop methods; `hover` shows the highlight without dropping.

## Chat data

The chat answers from one index of every living entry (`NoteIndex`, file `Application Support/Porridge/note-index.plist`). It is derived data: never synced, not part of the sync format, rebuilt from the notes at any time (deleting the file is safe). Code: `Porridge/Services/ChatDocument.swift` (entry, filters, context), `ChunkRanking.swift` (passages, BM25, vectors), `ChatService.swift` (passages of a note, prompt, sources), `NoteIndex.swift` (storage). Android mirrors it in `index/NoteIndex.kt`, `logic/NoteChunks.kt`, `logic/ChatLogic.kt`.

- **What is in it:** per note that is not in Recently Deleted (archived ones stay, marked) one entry (`ChatDocument`) and its passages (`ChatChunk`). Kept per note with a hash (`Note.indexHash`) over title, text, summary, page text, URL, labels, created date, transcript, speakers, attachments, channel (`sourceRaw`), buckets (`projectIDs`), `archivedAt` and the to-do fields (state, due day, done date, overdue); a note is cut again only when its hash changes. The index is refreshed before every question and 0.8 s after the note list changes (also after sync, which sets `updatedAt`), so creating, editing, filing, archiving, deleting and incoming sync all reach it.
- **Entry (`ChatDocument`):** `noteID`, `kind` (`todo` > `link` > `image` > `file` > `meeting` > `note`, the order of `Note.kindName`), `title` (`displayTitle`), `createdAt`, `bucketIDs` (`projectIDs`; empty = Inbox), `url`, `domain`, `todoState` ("open"/"done", nil otherwise), `dueDay` ("yyyy-MM-dd"), `doneAt`, `subtasksDone`/`subtasksTotal`, `archived` (`archivedAt` set; not a to-do done before today), `tags`, `snippet` (first plain lines without headings of summary, else text (without its first line when that is the title), else page text, else the first 20 transcript segments; at most 200 characters, cut with "…"). Bucket names are not stored: they are looked up per question (buckets get renamed).
- **Header** of an entry, the same for its list line and every passage of it: `«Title» — <Kind>[ (domain) for links][, open|done[ on <EEE d MMM yyyy>][, due <d MMMM yyyy>[, overdue]][, n of m subtasks done]] · created <EEE d MMM yyyy> · Bucket: A | Buckets: A, B | Inbox[ · #tag #tag][ · archived][ · <url>]`. Dates in English (`en_US_POSIX`), e.g. `Tue 17 Nov 2026`.
- **Passages (`ChatChunk`, field `part`):** `link` ("Saved link (domain): url"), `files` ("Image: …"/"File: …" names), `todo` (`todoLine`), `summary` ("Summary: …"), `text`, `page` ("Article (domain): …", "Text in the image: …", "Text of the file: …"), `transcript` ("Transcript: Speaker: …"), and `title` when there is nothing else. Cut at paragraph breaks into about 700 characters. Old files without `part` read it as `text`.
- **Question → filters (`ChatQuery.parse`):** words are the lowercased question split at everything but letters, digits and "-". Kind words (German and English, list in `ChatQuery.kindWords`): to-do (`todo(s)`, `to-do(s)`, `aufgabe(n)`, `task(s)`), link (`link(s)`, `lesezeichen`, `bookmark(s)`, `artikel`, `article(s)`, `webseite(n)`, `website(s)`), meeting (`meeting(s)`, `besprechung(en)`, `aufnahme(n)`, `recording(s)`, `transkript(e)`, `transcript(s)`, `call(s)`, `telefonat(e)`), image (`bild(er)`, `foto(s)`, `image(s)`, `photo(s)`, `screenshot(s)`), file (`datei(en)`, `file(s)`, `pdf(s)`, `dokument(e)`, `document(s)`); "note"/"Notiz" is no filter. State: open (`offen…`, `open`, `unerledigt…`, `ausstehend…`, `pending`, `anstehend…`), done (`erledigt…`, `done`, `completed`, `finished`, `abgehakt…`, `abgeschlossen…`, `geschafft`), overdue (`überfällig…`, `overdue`, `verspätet`, `late`). Due (`fällig…`, `due`, `deadline(s)`, `frist(en)`) makes the time about the due day. A bucket is named by its whole name as consecutive words; `inbox` means no bucket; `bucket(s)` is dropped. Time (`ChatQuery.timeRange`, first that fits, days `[start, end)` in the device calendar, weeks from Monday): a written date (`17.11.`, `17.11.2026`, `2026-11-17`); `[letzten|nächsten] N Tage|Wochen|Monate` (N digits or two…ten; back: `[today−N, tomorrow)`, ahead: `[today, today+N+1)`, a month counts 30 days); übermorgen, vorgestern, heute/today, gestern/yesterday, morgen/tomorrow; nächste/letzte/diese Woche; nächsten/letzten/diesen Monat; vague recent words (`letzten Tage`, `kürzlich`, `recently`, …) = the last 7 days and today; a weekday name = the last such day (today included), or the next one (today included) when the question is about a due day or open to-dos. Then: a due day or "overdue" adds kind to-do; a state without a kind means to-do; a question only about to-dos without a state lists open ones; done with a time is about the done date. Every word used for a filter is left out of the words searched for; the rest goes through the stop words (`ChatRetrieval.stopWords`).
- **Match (`ChatQuery.matches`):** kind in the kinds asked (any when none); in one of the buckets asked or, with `inbox`, in none; the state only applies to to-dos (overdue = open and due before today); the time is checked on the due day (`due`), the done date (`done`), or by default on the due day of a to-do with one, else the created date.
- **Context (`ChatRetrieval.context`):** without a filter, the best passages of all entries (`select`: BM25 plus vectors, at most 10 passages, 6500 characters, 3 per note; a named time first; the newest when no word fits). With a filter: (1) every matching entry as a list line (header and snippet), up to 40; open to-dos by due day (undated last), then done ones by done date, the rest newest first; to-dos first when the question is about them. When words are left to search for, entries that have them come first (most words first) and their line ends with `— mentions: w1, w2`. (2) The content passages (summary, text, page, transcript) of the matches that fit the words. (3) Further content passages (summary, text, page, transcript) of the first 8 matches in order, 12 per entry when there are at most 2 matches, 4 for at most 5, else 2, up to 6500 characters. (4) When words were left but no match has them, the best passages of the other entries (3000 characters), marked as outside. When nothing matches the filters at all, the model is told so and gets the best passages of all entries, marked as outside.
- **Prompt:** the system names today (`Today is <complete date> (EEE d MMM yyyy).`), says to answer only from the items and not to guess, to answer list questions in full from the list of matches and add nothing (for a topic, to take the entries that are about it), to write the answer in words (every entry by its title with the facts asked for, never numbers alone) with the numbers used after the facts like `[2]`, to answer in the language of the question, and to say plainly that nothing was found when the items do not hold the answer. The user part: the last 4 messages (500 characters each), then `Matching entries (<filters in words>): all N entries.` (or `the first M of N entries.` when cut) with `[n] <header>` and the snippet indented on the next line, or `No entry matches (<filters>).`; then `Excerpts from the matching entries:` (`Excerpts:` without filters) with `[n] <header>` and the passage; then `Excerpts from other entries (…)`; then `Question: …`. Numbers run through list lines, passages and outside passages in that order. The Apple model gets at most 7000 characters, half of them for the list.
- **Sources:** the notes behind the numbers the answer quotes; with no number, the first three passages (not list lines).
